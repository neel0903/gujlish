About

      
Gujlish . Predictive text for romanised Gujarati.
        Everything runs on this device and nothing is sent anywhere.
        Word frequencies derive from Gujarati Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0), the Dakshina dataset
        (CC BY-SA 4.0) and Aksharantar (CC BY / CC0); English frequencies from
        FrequencyWords (MIT).
